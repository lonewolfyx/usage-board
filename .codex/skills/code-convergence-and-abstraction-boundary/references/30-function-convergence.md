# Function Logic Convergence

## Applicability

Use this reference for repeated decisions, repeated transformations, repeated validation, repeated formatting, and repeated error handling.

## Best Practices

1. Converge pure calculation logic into pure functions.
2. Converge stable multi-branch mappings into strategy maps.
3. Converge field rules into validators.
4. Converge display transformations into formatters.
5. Converge API response shaping into normalizers, parsers, or mappers.

## Do Not

1. Do not create thin wrappers in the name of convergence.
2. Do not give helpers names that are more abstract than the business rule.
3. Do not hide business differences behind opaque parameters.
4. Do not force different business semantics into one function.
